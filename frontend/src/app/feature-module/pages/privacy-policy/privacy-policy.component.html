Privacy Policy

Introduction

This privacy policy outlines how Hôtel-Dieu de France (HDF) uses and safeguards the information provided by users. HDF is committed to ensuring the highest level of protection for personal data. By accessing HDF | ClinDoctor®, users acknowledge and agree to the terms outlined in this privacy policy.

Information We Collect

  • Name
  • Contact information, including email address, postal address, and phone number

Collected information is used to understand user needs and provide better service.

Content Accuracy

The HDF | ClinDoctor® application aims to provide accurate and up-to-date content. Users assume responsibility for applying the information and accept the risks associated with downloading files.

Security Measures

We are committed to ensuring the best possible security for user data. Appropriate technical and managerial procedures are implemented to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure.

Transmission of Data to Third Parties

To provide our services, HDF may share data with contracted parties for accounting, IT, and database support. All shared data remains protected under this policy, and companies comply with applicable data protection legislation.

Data Retention

HDF collects and retains user data for the purpose of providing requested services.

Cookies

Cookies are used to enhance browsing experience and functionality, including login memory, language preferences, and personalization.

Links to Other Websites

HDF’s application may contain external links. Once you leave our application, we are not responsible for the privacy practices of other websites. Users should review the privacy policies of external sites.

Control of Personal Information

Users may restrict the collection or use of their personal information. HDF will only use personal information to contact users when necessary to deliver services.

Refund Policy
  • Full Refund: Appointments canceled at least 24 hours prior to the scheduled time.
  • Partial Refund: Cancellations between 24 hours and 1 hour before the scheduled time (50% refund).
  • No Refund: Cancellations made less than 1 hour before the scheduled appointment.
  • Rescheduling: Users may reschedule appointments without additional fees.
  • Cancellation by Doctor: Full refund and rescheduling option at no extra cost.
Acceptance of Terms

By continuing to use HDF | ClinDoctor®, users signify acceptance of the terms and conditions stated in this privacy policy. This policy may be updated periodically, and users are encouraged to review it regularly.

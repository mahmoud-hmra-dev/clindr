### How to expose Clindoctor Call with Ngrok?

Expose your Clindoctor Call instance through Ngrok by following the same tunnel steps described in the upstream P2P guide: [https://docs.mirotalk.com/mirotalk-p2p/ngrok/](https://docs.mirotalk.com/mirotalk-p2p/ngrok/). Replace the app name and domain with your Clindoctor Call host.
